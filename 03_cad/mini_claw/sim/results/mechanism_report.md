## 2. End-stops (driving past the design range)

- **Closing past 0°:** first contact at -0.5° (right jaw / left jaw).
- **Opening past 30°:** no contact up to 45°.
- **Gears stay in mesh** over the whole sweep (-2° to 44°).

At SG90 no-load speed (0.1 s per 60°) a full stroke takes about **75 ms**, and the tips move at about **214 mm/s**. That's fast enough to pinch a finger, so close slowly with SPEED in the firmware.

## Limits

- Kinematic only: no forces, friction, bending or wear. Teeth are perfect CAD shapes; printed teeth will be slightly different.
- Parts are checked in three height bands, using the heights in `mini_claw.scad` (which include estimated servo dimensions, see MEASURE).
- The pinion screw head is assumed to be 4 mm across. Measure the real screw.
- The servo case is a box keep-out; the real case is slightly smaller.
